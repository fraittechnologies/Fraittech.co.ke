## What Each SEO Element Does

### Meta Title
- **What it is:** The clickable headline in Google search results
- **Why it matters:** Determines if people click your result
- **Target:** 50-60 characters

### Meta Description  
- **What it is:** The text preview under your title in search results
- **Why it matters:** Convinces people to click
- **Target:** 155-160 characters

### Keywords
- **What it is:** Words that describe your page content
- **Why it matters:** Tells Google what your page is about
- **Target:** 5-10 relevant keywords per page

### Open Graph Tags
- **What it is:** Tags for social media sharing
- **Why it matters:** Makes your link look better on Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter
- **Benefits:** More clicks, better branding

### Canonical URL
- **What it is:** Your official page URL
- **Why it matters:** Prevents Google from penalizing you for duplicate content
- **Benefit:** All ranking power goes to one URL

### Structured Data (JSON-LD)
- **What it is:** Code that tells Google what your business is
- **Why it matters:** Helps Google show rich snippets and understand your business
- **Benefit:** Better appearance in search results

### robots.txt
- **What it is:** File that controls crawler access
- **Why it matters:** Tells Google which pages to crawl
- **Benefit:** Better crawl efficiency

### Sitemap.xml
- **What it is:** Map of all your pages
- **Why it matters:** Helps Google find all pages faster
- **Benefit:** Faster indexing of new pages

## Key Metrics to Track

### Impressions
- How many times your page appeared in search results
- **Target:** Growing week-over-week

### Clicks  
- How many people clicked from Google to your site
- **Target:** Growing week-over-week

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Percentage of impressions that become clicks
- **Target:** Above 3%

### Average Position
- Your average ranking position (1-100)
- **Target:** Below 30 (page 1-2 of Google)

## Common Questions

### Q: How long until I see results?
**A:** 1-2 weeks for indexing, 1-3 months for traffic, 6 months for significant growth.

### Q: Do I need to pay for better rankings?
**A:** No. Organic (free) rankings come from quality content and links. Google Ads is separate (paid).

### Q: Will my site be indexed automatically?
**A:** Eventually yes, but submitting sitemap to Google Search Console speeds it up to 1-2 weeks.

### Q: What if my pages don't get indexed?
**A:** Check robots.txt isn't blocking them, ensure no noindex tags, and check for errors in Google Search Console.

### Q: How many pages should be indexed?
**A:** At minimum, all 8 main pages should be indexed.

### Q: What's the difference between submitted and indexed?
**A:** **Submitted** = you told Google about it. **Indexed** = Google crawled it and added to its database. Indexed is what matters for rankings.
